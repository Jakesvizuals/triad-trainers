    COMMUNITY SAFETY

    GO Lounge moderation and access controls

    Age-based chat routing

    • Adults are routed into adults-only rooms.
    • Underage users are routed into kid-safe rooms.
    • General social chat remains available for verified community members.

    Automated moderation

    • Filters scan for profanity, hate speech, racial slurs, and vulgar content.
    • Flagged content is logged, recorded, and sent to admin review.
    • Repeat offenders can be warned, muted, or removed.

    Community terms, safety rules, and privacy notice

    This community framework is designed to support safe Pokémon GO trainer conversations, protect minors, and give admins the tools to enforce policies. It is an original safety policy template for the site and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before launch in your jurisdiction.

    • Users must follow the site rules and treat others with respect.
    • Harassment, hate speech, racism, slurs, and explicit content are not tolerated.
    • Admins may remove users, mute accounts, and review reports when needed.
    • Records of content review and moderation actions may be retained for safety and compliance purposes.
